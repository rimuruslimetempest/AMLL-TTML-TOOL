# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Assume good intent, give feedback about the work rather than the person, and make room for people with different levels of experience and different languages.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Crediting other people's work accurately.
- Respecting privacy and not sharing another person's lyrics, audio, account details, or private messages.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private information, or deliberately disrupting the project.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to **rimurusamllttmltool@gmail.com**. Include what happened and any links or screenshots you are comfortable sharing. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful. Enforcement decisions will be proportionate and explained where practical.
